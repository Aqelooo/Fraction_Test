How Fraction Works

Earn cashback without connecting bank accounts, credit cards, or handling complicated payment gateways.

1

Shop & Show ID/QR

Visit any participating merchant across Jordan (Amman, Zarqa, Irbid, etc.). Pay cash or however you prefer, and simply have the cashier scan your Fraction QR or note your User ID.

2

Instant Cashback

A percentage of your bill is instantly credited to your Fraction app wallet in real Jordanian Dinars (JOD). No delays, points tricks, or complex vouchers.

3

Redeem in 2 Ways

Use your accumulated cashback to pay for purchases across our network, or cash out physically to your bank/e-wallet via CliQ or authorized physical cash agents.

1. Pay at Partner Stores

Use your Fraction balance like real money to pay fully or partially for coffee, dining, groceries, fashion, and tech within our partner network.

2. CliQ or Cash Agent Withdrawal

Want hard cash? Instantly transfer your earnings via CliQ alias to any Jordanian bank account, or pick up physical cash at authorized local Fraction agents.
